01 — The difference

Every number on this site is traceable to a file

Most portfolios assert impact. This one cites it. Each figure below was extracted programmatically from a reports/*.json produced by a pipeline in a public repository — not typed by hand, and not rounded in my favour.

01

Ground truth is injected, then scored against

Each generator plants a known mechanism — a fraud ring, a concept drift, a causal effect. The analysis is graded against what was actually planted. "It looks right" is never the standard.

02

A suspiciously good number is a bug report

A 100% score triggers an investigation. A PII detector scoring 1.000 fell to 0.20 on ten hand-written cases — and the honest figure is the one that ships.

03

Refuted hypotheses are reported as results

Event-time windowing was supposed to beat processing time. It discarded 95.7% of the stream. The measurement is the finding, not the hypothesis.

04

Reproducibility is measured, not claimed

Datasets are verified byte-identical by SHA-256 in a fresh process — because two projects shipped fixed seeds that still weren't deterministic.
